Field note 01Systems / architecture

Design around what changes.

Good architecture lets product areas change at different speeds. Find the real business boundaries first, then add the simplest seams that keep them independent.

Working takeaway

Isolate the most volatile workflow first. A small, clear boundary is more useful than adding layers everywhere.
